# LUMEN BOOSTED SYSTEM V2 — EXECUTIVE PLAN

Status: ARCHITECTURE CANDIDATE
Goal: make LUMEN a living digital artwork whose technology disappears behind a coherent sanctuary experience.

## Product law

LUMEN is not an AI chat app.

Its canonical experience loop is:

**VOIR → COMPRENDRE → AGIR → RETOUR → TRANSMETTRE**

Artificial intelligence is the backstage production and intelligence system. It must not turn the sanctuary into an AI dashboard.

## Governing architecture

One Governor routes work to specialist desks. Specialists do not publish directly.

Human Canon Authority
→ Trust & Gates
→ LUMEN Governor
→ Specialist desks
→ Tools/connectors
→ Evidence ledger
→ Independent reviewer
→ Human Gate
→ Canon/Production

## Critical rule

No provider is "the winner" forever.

For Golden assets:
1. create a provider-neutral SceneSpec,
2. render multiple candidates,
3. evaluate visual fidelity and artifacts,
4. independent review,
5. Enzo chooses the canonical winner.

This preserves LUMEN even when model rankings change.
